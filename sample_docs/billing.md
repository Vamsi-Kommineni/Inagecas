# Billing and plans

## Plans

Acme Cloud has three plans: Free, Team, and Enterprise. The Free plan includes 1
workspace and 3 members. The Team plan adds unlimited members, priority support,
and usage-based scaling. Enterprise adds SSO, audit logs, and a dedicated
support contact.

## Changing your plan

Workspace owners can upgrade or downgrade from **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades
take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of
the current billing period.

## Payment methods

We accept major credit cards. Annual plans can also be paid by invoice. Update
your card from **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

## Refunds

Monthly plans are non-refundable. For annual plans, you may request a prorated
refund within 30 days of purchase by contacting support.
